## New / expanded claims in the 2026 appearance
The following is a list of new information gleaned in this appearence compared to his [2019 appearence on Joe Rogan's Podcast](../rogan/).

### 1) Two explicit “directives” (new detail)
In 2026 Lazar says he was given **two directives**:
- **Directive 1:** duplicate the technology “with available material at any cost.”
- **Directive 2:** **disable the technology at a distance** “at any cost.”

### 2) Material/engineering details about the craft’s “waveguide pipe” (new technical description)
2026 adds a more specific description of a **retracting pipe/waveguide** that:
- retracts “like an antenna” *but without sections*,
- gets “smaller” without bulging/thickening,
- bends/turns with no visible mechanism/wires,
- and is framed as essentially “magical material behavior.”

### 3) “Insulator ring” + voltage measurement (new detail)
2026 introduces an “**insulator ring**” / “first ripple” on the exterior:
- Lazar says they **measured high voltage** on the craft,
- **below** that ring there was high voltage “all the time,”
- **above** the ring there wasn’t.

### 4) Count of personnel at S4 (new numeric claim)
2026: Lazar claims **“22 people total”** at the site (including himself).

### 5) New “project might have moved” anecdote (Kwajalein/South Pacific)
2026: Lazar says Barry talked about possibly moving the project to the **South Pacific (Kwajalein)** but it was too expensive.

### 6) Expanded “disinformation” theme: “humans are containers” / “containers of souls”
2026 spends time on a document-claim thread:
- “humans are containers,” with speculation about “souls,” plus John Lear “soul catcher” story.

### 7) New “why Navy?” emphasis + water focus
2026: Lazar emphasizes a recurring question: **why the Navy paid him / why “it was the Navy”** and not Air Force.
He links it to **transmedium** themes and repeatedly asks: **“what’s the deal with the ocean?”**

### 8) Element 115 physical description + machining/quantity (more specific)
2026 adds concrete specifics:
- he says he **physically touched** 115,
- describes it as **copper-like** in color,
- says it was **already machined/cut** in the required wedge/stacked-disc geometry,
- and says they had **“quite a bit of it.”**

### 9) More explicit “anti-gravity field” observation: “cancels weight” vs “pushes off”
2026 expands the description:
- the reactor/field didn’t behave like magnetic repulsion (where the object would slide away),
- and he claims the craft wasn’t “pushing on the ground” but **“cancelling out its weight.”**

### 10) New alleged evidence/discoveries from Luigi’s research (not Lazar’s original story)
A large chunk of 2026 is Luigi presenting “finds,” including:
- a **1941 map** allegedly showing a road into the mountain where S4 would be, later removed from later maps,
- claims about **photos taken Dec 25, 2020** by a pilot that—after contrast adjustment—show geometric shapes consistent with hangar/doors,
- a claim that **Google Earth imagery changed June 22, 2024** with a “box” over Papoose Lake that looks like deliberate obscuring, but which “accentuates tracks.”

### 11) VR/CGI reconstruction triggers “recovered memories” (new meta-claim)
2026: both Bob and Luigi claim the reconstruction process caused Lazar to **remember additional layout details** (e.g., “wait there’s another door there,” etc.).
